myPrayers

A voice to your prayers…

For iPhone

Write your prayers once. Then listen, and pray along, while myPrayers reads them back to you.

What it does

Your lists, read aloud

Keep a list for each day, or each person. Press play and your prayers are spoken back to you, one at a time, for as long as you set.

Scripture along the way

Verses on prayer are read between your own, so the time is shaped by more than your own words.

Music underneath

Quiet instrumental music plays behind the voice. Choose a piece you like, or let it vary each time.

A record of answers

Mark a prayer answered and it moves to your archive with the date. Export the whole archive as a PDF whenever you want to look back.

Praying with your church

Join your church with an invite code to pray through lists the staff keeps, and send in requests of your own.
